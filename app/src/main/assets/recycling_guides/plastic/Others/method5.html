How to Make a CD Wind Chime

Other Plastics
Repurpose old, unwanted CDs or DVDs into a shimmering, musical wind chime for your garden or balcony. This project utilizes the natural reflective quality of the polycarbonate discs to create beautiful light patterns while upcycling plastic waste.

Materials Needed:

  • 6–10 old CDs or DVDs
  • Sturdy stick or an old clothes hanger
  • Fishing line or strong twine
  • Small drill or hot metal skewer
  • Beads or permanent markers (optional)

Steps:

1
Decorate the discs (optional). Use permanent markers to draw designs on the non-shiny side, or apply beads using strong glue for extra texture and color. Let dry.
2
Drill hanging holes. Carefully drill a small hole near the top edge of each CD. If you want a tiered look, drill a second hole directly opposite the first one on the bottom edge.
3
Prepare the base. Take your stick or plastic hanger. Cut several lengths of fishing line, varying the lengths (e.g., 10 to 20 inches) for a staggered musical effect.
4
Attach the discs. Thread the fishing line through the hole in a CD and tie a secure knot. If tiering, tie the next line to the bottom hole of the top CD. Repeat for all discs.
5
Hang from support. Tie the free ends of the fishing lines to the stick or hanger, spacing them out evenly so the discs can lightly strike each other in the wind.
6
Final setup. Attach a loop of twine to the top of the stick or hanger. Hang the completed chime in a breezy, sunny spot to maximize the light reflection and gentle sound.
