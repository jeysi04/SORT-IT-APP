Return Water Container for Professional Recycling

Other Plastics
Returning water containers to professional recycling systems is the safest and most environmentally responsible option, especially for large refillable containers (such as 5-gallon/20-liter water jugs). These containers are commonly made from polycarbonate or other durable plastics that require industrial recycling processes. Water refilling companies already have systems in place to collect, inspect, and send unusable containers to specialized recyclers—keeping them out of landfills and preventing unsafe reuse.

Materials Needed:

  • Containers still recognized by water refilling stations
  • Containers from known water suppliers or brands
  • Cracked, cloudy, expired, or no-longer-safe refillable jugs

Steps:

1
Check return eligibility. Look for brand labels and ask your local refilling station if the container must be returned to their specific facility.
2
Do not DIY recycle or reuse. Avoid cutting or heating the jug; damaged polycarbonate is entirely unsafe for food or water storage.
3
Rinse lightly. Empty the container completely and rinse the inside with clean water only. Do not use harsh chemicals.
4
Return to the supplier. Inform staff if the jug is damaged or expired, and return caps or seals if required by the supplier.
5
Professional sorting. Suppliers inspect containers—usable ones are refurbished, while damaged ones are sent to industrial recyclers for proper processing.
