DIY Drip Irrigation System using Old Water Jugs

Other Plastics
Old polycarbonate or thick plastic water jugs can be turned into a slow-release drip irrigation system for your garden. This is perfect for keeping plants hydrated during dry spells without wasting water.

Materials Needed:

  • Old water container (structurally sound)
  • Drill or a hammer and small nail
  • Trowel or small shovel

Steps:

1
Clean the container. Ensure the jug is free of any soap or chemical residue that could harm your garden plants.
2
Puncture the bottom. Use a drill or a hammer and nail to poke 3 to 4 tiny holes into the bottom of the water container.
3
Position the jug. Dig a shallow hole near the base of the plant you want to water and bury the bottom of the jug slightly to keep it stable.
4
Fill and monitor. Fill the jug with water and leave the cap slightly loose. The water will slowly seep into the soil, keeping the roots perfectly hydrated over several days.
