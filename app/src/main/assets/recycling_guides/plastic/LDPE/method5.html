Precision Oil Dispenser

LDPE Plastic
Transform a clean LDPE squeeze bottle into a precision oiler. This is ideal for lubricating household hinges, bicycle chains, or small mechanical parts where you need to apply oil one drop at a time.

Materials Needed:

  • LDPE Squeeze Bottle
  • Lubricating Oil
  • Needle or Pin

Steps:

1
Sanitize and dry. Wash the bottle with soap and warm water. It is essential that the bottle is completely dry before adding oil to prevent moisture from causing rust in your machines.
2
Create a micro-opening. Use a fine needle or pin to puncture the very tip of the nozzle. This ensures a slow, controlled drip rather than a large stream of oil.
3
Fill and store. Pour your lubricating oil into the bottle. Store it upright in a cool, dry place and use the fine nozzle to reach tight mechanical gaps.
