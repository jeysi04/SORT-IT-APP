Make DIY Waterproof Book Covers

LDPE sheets can be reused to create waterproof book covers that protect books from spills, dirt, and rain. This simple DIY project is especially useful for school books, notebooks, and frequently used manuals.

Materials Needed:

  • Clean LDPE sheets or plastic bags (cut open and flattened)
  • Tape or a heat-sealing tool (such as a warm iron with paper barrier)
  • Scissors

Steps:

  1. Prepare the plastic sheet. Clean the LDPE material and make sure it is completely dry. Cut open plastic bags to create a flat sheet if needed.
  2. Measure and cut. Place the book in the center of the plastic sheet. Cut the plastic so it is slightly larger than the book, leaving extra space on all sides for folding.
  3. Wrap the book. Lay the book flat and fold the plastic over the front and back covers, similar to wrapping a gift.
  4. Fold the edges neatly. Tuck the top, bottom, and side edges inward to create clean folds. Make sure the plastic fits snugly around the book without bending the pages.
  5. Secure the cover. Use tape to hold the folded edges in place. For a longer-lasting cover, lightly heat-seal the edges using a warm iron with a sheet of paper between the iron and plastic.
  6. Check the fit. Open and close the book to ensure the cover does not slip and that pages turn easily. Adjust if necessary.
