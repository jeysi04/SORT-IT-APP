DIY Paint Dispenser

LDPE Plastic
Transform an empty LDPE squeeze bottle into a convenient paint dispenser for your art projects. This method allows for controlled dispensing of paint, reducing massive spills and minimizing waste during your creative sessions.

Materials Needed:

  • Clean condiment squeeze bottle
  • Paint or liquid dye
  • Warm water and soap
  • Small funnel (optional)
  • Small glass marble (optional)

Steps:

1
Check the bottle. Inspect the LDPE bottle for weak spots or cracks. Ensure the cap still snaps or screws on tightly so your paint will not dry out while in storage.
2
Wash and dry. Clean the bottle with warm, soapy water to remove all food oils. Squeeze water through the nozzle to clear any blockages, then let it air dry completely.
3
Fill with paint. Use a small funnel to carefully pour your craft paint into the bottle. Leave a small air gap at the top to make squeezing the bottle easier.
4
Optional: Add a shaker. Drop a small glass marble inside the bottle. This acts as a shaker ball to help mix the paint if it begins to separate over time.
5
Dispense and store. Squeeze gently to dispense paint onto your palette. When finished, wipe the nozzle clean and cap it tightly to keep the paint fresh for your next use.
