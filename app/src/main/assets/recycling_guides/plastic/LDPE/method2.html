Make Jump Ropes

LDPE plastic bags can be reused by braiding them into strong, flexible cords. These DIY jump ropes are durable and useful not only for exercise but also for making mats, handles, or ties, helping reduce plastic waste in a creative way.

Materials Needed:

  • Clean LDPE plastic bags
  • Scissors
  • Optional: tape for reinforcing the ends

Steps:

  1. Clean and prepare the bags. Make sure the plastic bags are clean and dry. Remove any handles or labels if needed.
  2. Cut into long strips. Lay each bag flat and cut it into long, even strips. The wider the strips, the thicker and stronger the rope will be.
  3. Connect the strips. Tie the plastic strips end-to-end to create several long strands. Pull knots tight to prevent slipping.
  4. Braid or twist the rope. Group three or more long strands together and braid them tightly, or twist them in one direction for added strength. Keep tension even while working.
  5. Secure the ends. Tie strong knots at both ends of the rope. You may wrap tape around the ends for extra durability and comfort when holding.
  6. Test and use. Gently pull the rope to check strength. Use it as a jump rope, floor mat edging, bag handle, or general-purpose cord.
