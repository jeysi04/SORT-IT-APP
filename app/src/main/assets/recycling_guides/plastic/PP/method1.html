Reuse as Storage Containers

Polypropylene (PP) containers are durable, lightweight, and suitable for repeated non-food storage. Reusing PP containers helps reduce plastic waste while keeping small household and craft items neatly organized.

Materials Needed:

  • Clean PP containers with lids
  • Soap and water
  • Clean cloth or towel for drying

Steps:

  1. Check the recycling symbol. Look at the bottom of the container and confirm it has the recycling symbol #5 (PP) to ensure it is made from polypropylene.
  2. Wash thoroughly. Clean the container and lid with soap and water to remove any residue, grease, or dirt. Rinse well.
  3. Dry completely. Wipe the container dry or allow it to air-dry fully. This prevents moisture buildup and mold growth.
  4. Inspect for damage. Make sure the container has no cracks, warping, or sharp edges that could affect storage safety.
  5. Use for storage. Store small items such as screws, nails, buttons, beads, art supplies, or craft tools inside the container.
  6. Label if needed. Label the container for easy identification and better organization, especially when stacking multiple containers.
