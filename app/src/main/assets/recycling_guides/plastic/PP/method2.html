Make a Desk Organizer from Bottle Caps or Containers

Small polypropylene (PP) items such as bottle caps and small containers can be creatively reused to make desk organizers. This DIY project helps keep stationery and small tools organized while reducing plastic waste.

Materials Needed:

  • Clean PP bottle caps or small PP containers
  • Glue or hot glue
  • Cardboard base or any sturdy flat base (optional)
  • Clean cloth or towel

Steps:

  1. Clean and prepare the materials. Wash all PP bottle caps or containers with soap and water. Dry them completely to ensure proper adhesion.
  2. Plan the layout. Arrange the caps or containers on a flat surface or cardboard base. Test different layouts until you find one that suits your needs and available space.
  3. Attach the pieces. Apply glue or hot glue to the bottom of each cap or container. Press firmly onto the base or directly onto a flat surface. Hold for a few seconds to secure.
  4. Allow to dry. Let the glue dry completely according to the adhesive instructions. Avoid moving the organizer during this time.
  5. Check stability. Once dry, gently test each piece to make sure it is firmly attached and stable. Reapply glue if necessary.
  6. Use the organizer. Place pens, pencils, paper clips, USB drives, or small tools into the compartments to keep your desk neat.
