How to Build a Mini Compost Scoop

Old HDPE containers or jugs can be repurposed into handy scoops for compost, soil, or pet litter. This simple DIY project transforms waste plastic into a practical garden or household tool, helping reduce plastic waste while making chores easier.

Materials Needed:

  • Large HDPE container or jug (clean and intact)
  • Scissors or utility knife

Steps:

  1. Clean the container. Wash the HDPE container thoroughly to remove any residue. Let it dry completely.
  2. Plan the scoop shape. Decide which part of the container will become the scoop. Typically, the bottom or a side corner is ideal. Visualize an angled shape that will hold material efficiently.
  3. Cut the container. Using scissors or a utility knife, carefully cut the bottom or side at an angle to create the scoop. Smooth any sharp edges to prevent injury.
  4. Optional handle adjustment. If the handle of the jug is long or uncomfortable, you can trim or reposition it for better grip.
  5. Use your mini scoop. Use the scoop for handling compost, soil, sand, or pet litter. Its angled design allows you to easily scoop and pour materials where needed.
