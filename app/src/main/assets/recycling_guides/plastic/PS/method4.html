Create a Stamping Tool

Foam sheets can be easily transformed into custom stamping tools for arts and crafts. These DIY stamps are lightweight, easy to cut, and perfect for decorating paper, fabric, or other surfaces with creative patterns.

Materials Needed:

  • Foam sheet (clean and intact)
  • Scissors
  • Glue
  • Small wooden block or sturdy cardboard (for the base)
  • Ink pad or paint

Steps:

  1. Select and cut foam. Choose a clean foam sheet. Cut simple shapes such as stars, hearts, circles, or custom designs using scissors.
  2. Prepare the base. Use a small wooden block or piece of sturdy cardboard as the handle/base for your stamp. Ensure it fits comfortably in your hand.
  3. Attach the foam shape. Glue the foam shape onto the flat side of the base. Press firmly and let the glue dry completely.
  4. Test your stamp. Press the foam shape gently into an ink pad or paint, then stamp onto paper or fabric. Adjust pressure for a clear impression.
  5. Use your stamp. Repeat stamping to create patterns, decorate cards, wrapping paper, or DIY art projects. Clean the foam after use if needed to reuse.
