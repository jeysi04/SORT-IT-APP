DIY Jewelry Organizer from Plastic Cutleries

PS (Polystyrene) Plastic
Plastic cutlery, especially forks, can be creatively repurposed into a simple jewelry organizer. This DIY project is lightweight, easy to make, and perfect for hanging earrings, bracelets, and necklaces while keeping your jewelry organized.

Materials Needed:

  • Clean plastic forks
  • Small block of foam, cork, or sturdy cardboard
  • Glue (optional for extra stability)

Steps:

1
Clean the cutlery. Wash the plastic forks thoroughly with soap and water. Dry completely to ensure they stick or stay firmly in place.
2
Prepare the base. Choose a foam block, corkboard, or piece of cardboard as the base. Make sure it is stable enough to hold the jewelry without tipping over.
3
Insert the forks. Push the prongs of each fork into the foam or cardboard, spacing them evenly. Ensure they are secure and stand upright.
4
Optional reinforcement. For extra stability, apply a small amount of glue at the base of each fork before inserting it into the foam.
5
Drape bracelets and necklaces. Use the handle of the fork to drape bracelets, watches, or necklaces. Arrange items so they don’t overlap too much, making them easy to access.
6
Display and use. Place the jewelry organizer on a dresser, vanity, or shelf for daily use.
