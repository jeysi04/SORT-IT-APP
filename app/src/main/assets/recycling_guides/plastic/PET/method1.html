Create a Self-Watering Plant Bottle

PET Plastic
A self-watering plant bottle is an eco-friendly way to recycle plastic while keeping your plants hydrated. By cutting a single bottle in half, water is gradually drawn up into the soil. This is perfect for small indoor gardens or windowsills.

Materials Needed:

  • Plastic bottle (cleaned and dry)
  • Scissors or a craft knife
  • String, yarn, or a cloth strip
  • Soil
  • Seeds or small plants

Steps:

1
Prepare the bottle. Wash the bottle thoroughly and remove any labels. Make sure it is completely dry before cutting.
2
Cut the bottle. Using scissors or a craft knife, carefully cut the bottle around the middle to separate the top (planter) and bottom (reservoir) sections.
3
Create the reservoir. Fill the bottom half of the bottle with water. This will act as the continuous water supply for your self-watering system.
4
Invert the top half. Take the top half of the cut bottle and invert it so the neck points down into the bottom half. This section will hold your soil and plant.
5
Insert the wick. Thread a string or cloth strip through the bottle cap hole. The wick should hang down into the water, drawing it up into the soil via capillary action.
6
Add soil and plant. Fill the top half with potting soil, making sure the wick touches the soil. Plant your seeds or seedlings according to their instructions.
7
Monitor and refill. Place the planter in a sunny spot. Check the water level in the reservoir regularly and refill as needed to keep your plants happy.
