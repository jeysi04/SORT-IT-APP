Create a Hanging Herb Garden

PET Plastic
A hanging herb garden is a practical way to recycle plastic bottles while growing fresh herbs at home. This vertical garden saves space and keeps herbs within easy reach for cooking right on your balcony or kitchen window.

Materials Needed:

  • Plastic bottles (clean and dry)
  • Scissors or craft knife
  • String, rope, or twine
  • Soil
  • Herb seeds or small seedlings

Steps:

1
Clean the bottles. Wash the plastic bottles thoroughly to remove any residue and allow them to air dry completely before cutting.
2
Cut side openings. Using scissors or a craft knife, carefully cut a side opening in each bottle large enough to plant herbs. This is the front of your planter.
3
Create holes for hanging. Make two holes on opposite sides near the bottle's top to allow you to thread string or rope through for vertical hanging.
4
Thread the string. Thread string through the holes and tie knots below each bottle to hold them in place. You can stack several bottles to create a tower!
5
Fill with soil. Add potting soil into each bottle, making sure the soil reaches the opening for proper planting depth.
6
Plant herbs. Sow herb seeds or transplant small seedlings (like basil, parsley, mint, or thyme) directly into the soil.
7
Hang the garden. Securely hang the vertical bottle arrangement from a hook, rod, or railing in a sunny spot, ensuring it is stable.
8
Care for your plants. Water regularly to keep the soil moist but not waterlogged. Harvest fresh herbs as needed for your cooking!
