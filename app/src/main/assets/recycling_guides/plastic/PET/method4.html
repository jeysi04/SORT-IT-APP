Make Bird or Animal Water Stations

Plastic bottles can be recycled into simple water stations for birds or small animals. This DIY project provides a safe, easy-to-maintain water source while reusing plastic. It’s perfect for gardens, balconies, or even indoor spaces for small pets.

Materials Needed:

  • Plastic bottle (clean and dry)
  • Scissors or craft knife
  • String, twine, or a hook for hanging
  • Shallow tray or dish (optional, for stability)

Steps:

  1. Clean the bottle. Wash and dry thoroughly to prevent contamination.
  2. Cut the bottom. Remove the base and smooth the edges to avoid injury.
  3. Add water. Fill with fresh, clean water.
  4. Set it up. Hang with string or place on a stable tray where animals can reach it easily.
  5. Maintain it. Refill and clean regularly, especially during hot weather.
  6. Optional decoration. Use non toxic materials only to keep animals safe.
