Make Bird or Animal Water Stations

PET Plastic
Plastic bottles can be recycled into simple water stations for birds or small animals. This project provides a safe, easy-to-maintain water source for wildlife or indoor pets while reusing plastic waste.

Materials Needed:

  • Plastic bottle (clean and dry)
  • Scissors or craft knife
  • String, twine, or a hook for hanging
  • Shallow tray or dish (optional)

Steps:

1
Clean the bottle. Wash and dry the plastic bottle thoroughly to prevent contamination and keep the animals healthy.
2
Cut the bottom. Remove the base of the bottle with your scissors and use a file or sandpaper to smooth the edges to avoid injury to wildlife.
3
Add water. Fill the modified bottle with fresh, clean water. If using a shallow tray attachment, ensure water easily dispenses into the tray.
4
Set it up. Hang the station with string from a tree branch, or place it on a stable tray where small animals can reach it easily.
5
Maintain it. Check the station daily to refill and clean it regularly, especially during hot weather when algae can form quickly.
6
Optional: Decorate. Use only non-toxic materials if you decide to decorate the exterior to keep the visiting birds and animals safe!
