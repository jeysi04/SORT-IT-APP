Make a Small Planter or Seed Starter

Used Beverage Cartons
Used beverage cartons can be repurposed into small planters or seed starters, giving them a second life while helping your plants grow. This is a simple, eco-friendly way to start seedlings indoors or on a balcony.

Materials Needed:

  • Used beverage cartons (cleaned and dry)
  • Scissors
  • Soil
  • Seeds or small plants
  • Tape or glue (optional)
  • Paint, stickers, or markers for decoration (optional)

Steps:

1
Rinse the carton thoroughly. Wash the beverage carton inside and out to remove any leftover liquid or residue. Let it dry completely before proceeding.
2
Cut off the top. Using scissors, cut the top portion of the carton to create an open container. A height of about 10–15 cm is ideal for starting seedlings.
3
Create drainage holes. Poke 2–3 small holes in the bottom of the carton to allow excess water to drain. This prevents waterlogging and potential root rot.
4
Fill with soil. Add potting soil to the carton, leaving about 1–2 cm of space from the top. Press down lightly on the surface to settle the soil properly.
5
Plant seeds or seedlings. Place your seeds or small plants in the soil according to their specific package instructions. Water gently to moisten the soil throughout.
6
Position your planter. Place the planter on a windowsill or balcony that receives adequate sunlight to help your young plants thrive.
7
Optional: Decorate. Customize the outside of your carton planter using paint, stickers, or markers to give it a personal and colorful touch.
8
Care and transplant. Water regularly. Once the seedlings are large enough, you can transplant them directly into your garden, as the carton will eventually break down.
