Make Gift Wrapping

Selected White Ledger Paper
Selected White Ledger sheets can be turned into eco-friendly wrapping or craft paper, giving old paper a second life while adding a clean, sophisticated, and personal touch to your gifts and creative projects.

Materials Needed:

  • Clean white ledger sheets
  • Scissors or paper cutter
  • Ruler (optional)
  • Markers, stamps, paint, or colored pens (optional)

Steps:

1
Select clean sheets. Choose pages that are free from ink stains, tears, or heavy folds. Using high-quality sheets ensures a more professional and polished appearance for your gift.
2
Flatten the sheets. Smooth out any existing creases or wrinkles. You can press the sheets under a heavy book or iron them briefly on a very low heat setting (optional).
3
Cut sheets to size. Measure the item you intend to wrap and trim the ledger paper accordingly. Use a ruler for perfectly straight edges to ensure the wrap fits snugly.
4
Decorate the paper (optional). Add custom designs using markers, stamps, or paint. This is your chance to create unique patterns, elegant doodles, or specific themed artwork.
5
Fold or wrap. Wrap the gift carefully, making sure to fold the edges neatly. Secure the paper with a small piece of tape or a rustic piece of string as needed.
6
Explore other uses. This decorated ledger paper can also be repurposed for other creative projects like scrapbooking, handmade card-making, or various paper-based crafts.
