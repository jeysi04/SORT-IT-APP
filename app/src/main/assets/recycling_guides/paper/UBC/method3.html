Use Beverage Cartons as Storage or Organizer

Used Beverage Cartons
Used beverage cartons can be transformed into practical storage containers for small items around the house. They are perfect for organizing craft supplies, office materials, or hardware while keeping your space tidy and eco-friendly.

Materials Needed:

  • Beverage cartons (clean and dry)
  • Scissors
  • Glue or tape (optional)
  • Markers, stickers, or paint for decoration (optional)

Steps:

1
Clean the cartons. Rinse the inside of the beverage cartons thoroughly and allow them to dry completely. This ensures your storage remains clean and hygienic for your items.
2
Decide on the opening. Depending on what you want to store, you can leave the top open, cut it lower, or cut a side opening for easier access. Use scissors to make smooth edges.
3
Individual storage. Place small items like buttons, beads, screws, or office supplies into each carton. Each carton can hold a different type of item for easy classification.
4
Optional: Create a larger organizer. Group multiple cartons together by gluing or taping the sides. This creates a multi-compartment organizer perfect for desk drawers or craft tables.
5
Optional: Decorate. Personalize your storage cartons with markers, stickers, or paint to make them colorful and match the aesthetic of your workspace or room.
6
Arrange and use. Place your organized cartons on shelves or inside drawers. You can easily refill and rearrange them as needed to maintain an efficient storage system.
