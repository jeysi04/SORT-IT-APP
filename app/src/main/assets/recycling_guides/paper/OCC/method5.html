How to Make a Pet House using Corrugated Cardboard

Old Corrugated Cartons
Turn old cardboard boxes into a cozy, DIY pet house for your cat or small dog. Using recycled materials keeps your project eco-friendly while giving your pet a fun and private space to rest.

Materials Needed:

  • Old corrugated cardboard boxes
  • Scissors or box cutter
  • Ruler or measuring tape
  • Pencil or marker
  • Glue, tape, or hot glue gun
  • Fabric scraps, newspaper, or cushion (optional)

Steps:

1
Select suitable boxes. Check each box for sturdiness and cleanliness. Avoid boxes with water damage. Choose one large box for the main structure.
2
Decide the size. Measure your pet to ensure the house is spacious. Leave enough height and width for your pet to enter, turn, and lie down comfortably.
3
Prepare the entrance. Draw the shape of an entrance on the front of the large box. Carefully cut it out and smooth sharp edges to prevent injury.
4
Reinforce the structure. Glue extra pieces of cardboard inside the base and sides if the walls are thin. This helps the house stay sturdy and durable.
5
Add a roof. Fold the box flaps down to create a slanted roof, or attach a separate piece of cardboard on top using strong glue or tape.
6
Add ventilation (optional). Cut small windows on the sides for airflow, or add a second smaller box inside as a cozy internal compartment.
7
Add bedding. Line the floor with fabric scraps, newspaper, or a small cushion to make the internal environment soft and comfortable for your pet.
8
Decorate (optional). Use non-toxic markers or fabric to personalize the house, ensuring all decorations are pet-safe and securely attached.
9
Check stability. Before letting your pet inside, gently test the house’s stability. Ensure there are no loose edges, exposed tape, or sharp points.
10
Introduce your pet. Place the pet house in a quiet area. Encourage exploration by adding familiar treats or toys to make the space inviting.
