How to Make a Lamp from Carton

Old Corrugated Cartons
Recycle old cardboard boxes into a custom, decorative lamp for your home. This project is perfect for creating a unique light fixture while giving cardboard a second life as functional art.

Materials Needed:

  • Sturdy corrugated cardboard
  • Scissors or box cutter
  • Ruler
  • Pencil or marker
  • Glue, hot glue gun, or double-sided tape
  • Low-heat LED bulb or tea light
  • Cutting mat or protective surface
  • Decorative paper, markers, or paint (optional)
  • Craft knife (optional)

Steps:

1
Prepare the cardboard. Ensure it is clean, dry, and undamaged. Flatten the box and remove any staples or tape for a smooth working surface.
2
Plan the lamp design. Sketch your design (cube, cylinder, or lantern) and choose the size based on the dimensions of your LED light source.
3
Measure and mark. Mark panels for all sides using a ruler and pencil. Add optional cutout patterns to create interesting light and shadow effects.
4
Cut the panels. Cut along the marked lines and trim rough edges. Use a craft knife for more detailed or intricate cutouts on the panels.
5
Assemble the lamp body. Glue or tape the panels together to form the structure. Reinforce the internal corners if the design needs extra stability.
6
Create a light opening. Cut a hole or slot at the base or back for inserting and removing the LED light source easily.
7
Add a base (optional). Attach a weighted cardboard base to the bottom for extra stability and to prevent the lamp from tipping over.
8
Decorate. Paint or cover the lamp with decorative paper as desired. Let all adhesives and paints dry completely before continuing.
9
Insert the LED light. Use only low-heat LED lights. It is important to ensure the light source does not directly touch the cardboard.
10
Test and display. Check the stability and the lighting effect in a dark room, then place your recycled lamp in its final spot.
