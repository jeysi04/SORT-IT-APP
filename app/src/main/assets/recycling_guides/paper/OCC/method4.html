How to Make Picture Frames from Old Cardboard

Old paper bags can be transformed into durable and eco-friendly book covers. These covers protect books from dirt and wear while giving you a personalized surface to decorate. This is a simple recycling project that is perfect for schoolbooks, notebooks, or even novels.

Materials Needed:

  • Paper bags (clean and sturdy)
  • Scissors
  • Tape or glue
  • Markers, stickers, or stamps (optional, for decoration)
  • Ruler and pencil (optional, for precise folding)

Steps:

  1. Prepare the paper bag. Flatten the paper bag on a clean, flat surface. Smooth out any wrinkles or creases for easier cutting and folding.
  2. Cut open the bag. Cut along one side and the bottom seam of the bag to create a single flat sheet. You now have a piece of paper large enough to wrap around a book.
  3. Position the book. Place the book in the center of the paper sheet with the spine aligned along the center. Make sure there is enough paper on all sides to fold over the edges.
  4. Fold around the book. Fold the top and bottom edges over the front and back covers, then fold the left and right sides over the spine. Adjust the folds so the cover fits snugly but not too tight.
  5. Secure the edges. Use tape or a thin layer of glue to secure any overlapping edges. Make sure all folds are held firmly so the cover stays in place.
  6. Decorate the cover. Add designs, stickers, stamps, or write your name on the cover. This step is optional but makes your book unique and personalized.
  7. Use the book cover. Slip the book inside the newly made cover and enjoy a protective, stylish, and recycled book wrap.
