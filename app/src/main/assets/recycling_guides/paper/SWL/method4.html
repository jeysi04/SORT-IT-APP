How to Make a DIY Desk Notepad

Selected White Ledger Paper
Repurpose high-quality ledger paper into a functional desk notepad. This project is a great way to use clean, unprinted ledger sheets to organize your daily tasks while reducing office waste and creating something useful.

Materials Needed:

  • Stack of Selected White Ledger sheets
  • Cardboard scrap for the backing
  • White craft glue or wood glue
  • Two heavy binder clips
  • Ruler and utility knife
  • Decorative washi tape (optional)

Steps:

1
Prepare the paper stack. Gather your clean ledger sheets and align them perfectly. Use a ruler and utility knife to cut them into smaller, uniform sizes, such as 4 x 6 inches.
2
Cut the backing. Find a piece of sturdy cardboard and cut it to the exact same dimensions as your paper stack. This will act as the rigid base for your notepad.
3
Align and clamp. Place the paper stack on top of the cardboard base. Tap the top edge against a flat surface to make it perfectly flush, then clamp it with binder clips.
4
Apply the padding glue. Spread a thick, even layer of white craft glue across the top edge of the stack. Ensure the glue covers the entire width of the paper and the cardboard.
5
Let it cure. Allow the glue to dry completely while the clips are still attached. This typically takes about 30 to 60 minutes. Apply a second coat for extra durability.
6
Optional: Finish and decorate. Once the glue is dry, remove the clips. You can cover the glued top edge with colorful washi tape to give the notepad a more finished look.
