How to Make Gift Wrapping

Selected White Ledger sheets can be turned into eco-friendly wrapping or craft paper, giving old paper a second life while adding a personal touch to gifts and projects.

Materials Needed:

  • Clean white ledger sheets
  • Scissors or paper cutter
  • Ruler (optional, for precise cuts)
  • Markers, stamps, paint, or colored pens (optional)

Steps:

  1. Select clean sheets. Choose pages that are free from stains, tears, or heavy folds for the best appearance.
  2. Flatten the sheets. Smooth out any creases or wrinkles by pressing the sheets under a heavy book or ironing briefly on low heat (optional).
  3. Cut sheets to size. Measure and trim the paper to fit the gift or project. Use a ruler for straight edges if desired.
  4. Decorate the paper (optional). Add designs with markers, stamps, paint, or drawings. You can create patterns, doodles, or themed artwork.
  5. Fold or wrap. Wrap the gift carefully, folding edges neatly. Secure with tape or string as needed.
  6. Use for other projects. The decorated paper can also be used for scrapbooking, card-making, or other creative crafts.
