How to Make Newspaper Seedling Pots

Old Newspapers
Repurpose old newspapers into biodegradable seedling pots. These are eco-friendly alternatives to plastic starters because you can plant the entire pot directly into the soil, reducing transplant shock for your young plants.

Materials Needed:

  • Sheets of old newspaper
  • A small glass or jar (to use as a mold)
  • Potting soil
  • Seeds
  • Scissors (optional)

Steps:

1
Prepare the strips. Fold a full sheet of newspaper in half lengthwise twice, then cut or tear it into long strips roughly 5 inches wide.
2
Roll the mold. Lay the jar on its side at one end of the strip. Roll the newspaper strip around the jar, leaving about 2 inches of paper overhanging the bottom.
3
Fold the base. Push the overhanging paper inward toward the center of the jar’s bottom. Press firmly against a flat surface to crease the base into place.
4
Secure the shape. Remove the jar carefully. The weight of the soil will help hold the pot together, so no glue or tape is needed for this project.
5
Fill and plant. Fill your new newspaper pot with damp potting soil and plant your seeds according to the depth requirements on the seed packet.
