Recycle Old/New Paper

Old Newspapers
Turn old newspapers, notebooks, or office paper into homemade recycled paper using simple items you can find at home. This process helps reduce household waste while creating unique, textured paper for crafts or stationery.

Materials Needed:

  • Old newspapers or office paper
  • Clean water
  • Large bowl or bucket
  • Paper shredder or scissors (optional)

Steps:

1
Sort your paper. Go through your stack of old newspapers or notebooks. Remove any staples, plastic windows from envelopes, or adhesive tape to avoid contamination.
2
Tear or shred. Tear the paper by hand into small, confetti-sized pieces. Alternatively, use a paper shredder to speed up the process and ensure uniform sizes.
3
Soak in water. Place the paper pieces into a large bowl or bucket. Cover them with clean water and let them soak for a few hours until the paper becomes soft pulp.
4
Prepare for collection. Once the pulp is ready, you can drain the excess water and place it in a recycling bin or take it to your local community recycling center.
5
Dry for reuse. If making new sheets at home, spread the pulp thinly on a screen or flat surface. Let it dry completely in the sun before using it for your projects.
