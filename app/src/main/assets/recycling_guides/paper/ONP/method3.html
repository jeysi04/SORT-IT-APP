Reuse Old/New Paper

Turn old newspapers into eco-friendly gift bags that are unique, creative, and completely recyclable. This DIY project is perfect for small presents and craft fun.

Materials Needed:

  • Old newspapers, magazines, or scrap paper
  • Scissors, glue, tape
  • Markers, paint, or decorative items

Steps:

  1. Create envelopes or gift wrap from old magazines or newspapers.
  2. Make origami figures or paper flowers.
  3. Use scrap paper for notes, shopping lists, or practice sheets.
  4. Decorate notebooks or bind scrap paper into a DIY notebook.
