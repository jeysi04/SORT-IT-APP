How to Make a Bookmark Out of Old Folders

Mixed Paper
Bookmarks made from old folders are strong, reusable, and easy to customize. They are perfect for textbooks, notebooks, or novels and help to significantly reduce paper waste.

Materials Needed:

  • Old folder
  • Scissors
  • Marker or pen
  • Ruler and pencil (optional)
  • Hole punch and string or ribbon (optional)

Steps:

1
Prepare the folder. Flatten the folder and place it on a table to ensure clean and straight cuts during the process.
2
Measure bookmark size. Mark long strips on the folder, typically about 4–5 cm wide and 15–20 cm long.
3
Cut the bookmarks. Carefully cut along the lines to form long, thin strips, ensuring the edges are as straight as possible.
4
Round the corners. Trim the corners slightly to prevent curling or damage to the paper when placed inside your books.
5
Decorate the bookmark. Write quotes, reminders, or draw simple designs using markers or pens to personalize your bookmark.
6
Add a tassel (optional). Punch a hole at the top of the bookmark. Thread a string or ribbon through the hole and tie a secure knot.
7
Use the bookmark. Insert the finished bookmark into your books or notebooks to mark your place easily and style your reading.
