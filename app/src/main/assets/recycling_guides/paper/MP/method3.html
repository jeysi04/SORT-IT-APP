How to Make a Flashcards Out of Old Folders

Mixed Paper
Study flashcards made from old folders are sturdy, reusable, and perfect for reviewing lessons, definitions, or formulas. Since folder paper is thicker than regular paper, these cards are ideal for frequent studying.

Materials Needed:

  • Old folder (clean and flat)
  • Scissors
  • Marker or pen
  • Ruler and pencil
  • Optional: Rubber band or small box

Steps:

1
Prepare the folder. Lay the folder flat on a table. Make sure it is clean and free from heavy creases or tears for a smooth writing surface.
2
Measure the flashcard size. Lightly mark rectangles on the folder. A size of 7 × 10 cm is standard, but you may adjust this to your preference.
3
Cut the flashcards. Carefully cut along the marked lines to create evenly sized rectangles, ensuring each card is uniform for easier handling.
4
Round the corners. Trim the corners slightly to make them rounded. This helps prevent bending and makes the cards feel more professional.
5
Write study content. Write a term or question on the front and the definition, explanation, or answer on the back of each card.
6
Organize the flashcards. Group cards by subject. Secure them with a rubber band or place them in a small box or envelope for storage.
7
Use for studying. Review the flashcards regularly to test your memory and improve recall of key concepts before your next exam.
