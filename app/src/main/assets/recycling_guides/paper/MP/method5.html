How to Make a Flashcards Out of Old Folders

Study flashcards made from old folders are sturdy, reusable, and perfect for reviewing lessons, definitions, formulas, or key concepts. Since folder paper is thicker than regular paper, these flashcards last longer and are ideal for frequent studying.

Materials Needed:

  • Old folder (clean and flat)
  • Scissors
  • Marker or pen
  • Ruler and pencil (optional)
  • Rubber band or small box (optional, for storage)

Steps:

  1. Prepare the folder. Lay the folder flat on a table. Make sure it is clean and free from heavy creases or tears.
  2. Measure the flashcard size. Lightly mark rectangles on the folder. A good size is about 7 × 10 cm, but you may adjust depending on your preference.
  3. Cut the flashcards. Carefully cut along the marked lines to create evenly sized rectangles.
  4. Round the corners. Trim the corners slightly to make them rounded. This helps prevent bending and makes the cards easier to handle.
  5. Write study content. Front: write a term, question, or topic Back: write the definition, explanation, or answer
  6. Organize the flashcards. Group flashcards by subject or topic. Secure them with a rubber band or place them in a small box or envelope.
  7. Use for studying. Review the flashcards regularly to test your memory and improve recall.
