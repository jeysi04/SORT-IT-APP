How to Make a Placemats or Coasters

Mixed Paper
Placemats and coasters made from magazine pages are an easy and stylish way to recycle paper while adding color to your table. Glossy magazine patterns create eye-catching designs, and a cardboard backing ensures they are sturdy enough for daily use.

Materials Needed:

  • Magazine pages (clean and colorful)
  • Cardboard backing
  • Glue (white glue or craft glue)
  • Scissors or craft knife
  • Clear sealant or protective glue (optional)

Steps:

1
Plan the size and shape. Decide whether you are making rectangular placemats or smaller square/round coasters. Mark the dimensions on your cardboard.
2
Cut the cardboard backing. Carefully cut the cardboard to your desired shape. Ensure the edges are neat, as they will define the structure of your final product.
3
Prepare the magazine pages. Select pages with vibrant colors. Cut them into the same shape as the cardboard, or into smaller fragments for a mosaic collage.
4
Glue the paper in place. Apply an even layer of glue to the cardboard. Carefully place the paper on top, starting from one edge and smoothing it down as you go.
5
Smooth out wrinkles. Use your fingers or a flat object to gently press out air bubbles. This helps the paper stick evenly and ensures a clean, professional finish.
6
Trim excess paper. Once the glue has set slightly, trim any excess paper hanging over the edges of the cardboard for a tidy look.
7
Apply a protective layer. Brush a thin layer of clear sealant over the surface. This adds durability and helps protect against moisture and food stains.
8
Let it dry completely. Place the item on a flat surface and allow it to dry fully. Avoid using the placemats until the sealant is no longer tacky.
