How to Make Magazine Paper Baskets

Mixed Paper
Magazine paper baskets are a creative and practical way to recycle old magazines into useful storage items. The colorful prints add a decorative touch, while weaving the paper strips creates a surprisingly sturdy basket for stationery or craft supplies.

Materials Needed:

  • Old magazine pages (clean and colorful)
  • Scissors
  • Glue or tape
  • Ruler and pencil
  • Flat surface for weaving

Steps:

1
Select and prepare magazine pages. Choose full magazine pages with bright colors and minimal tears. Flatten the pages to remove folds or creases for easier cutting.
2
Cut the pages into strips. Using scissors and a ruler, cut the pages into long strips of equal width, about 2–3 cm wide. Uniform strips help create an even basket.
3
Fold the strips for strength. Fold each strip lengthwise in half, then fold again if needed, pressing firmly. This makes the strips strong enough for weaving.
4
Create the basket base. Lay several folded strips vertically on a flat surface. These strips will form the foundational base of your basket.
5
Start weaving horizontally. Take another strip and weave it horizontally across the vertical strips, going over one strip and under the next.
6
Build and tighten the base. Add more horizontal strips, pushing each one tightly against the previous strip. A tighter weave ensures a more stable basket.
7
Secure the base edges. Once the base reaches your desired size, apply small amounts of glue or tape at the edges to prevent the strips from shifting.
8
Form the basket sides. Carefully fold the ends of the vertical strips upward at a right angle along the edges of the base to create the framework for the walls.
9
Weave the side walls. Weave additional strips around the upright vertical strips, following the over-and-under pattern. Tighten layer by layer as you go around.
10
Secure and finish. When the basket reaches the desired height, glue or tape the ends of the woven strips neatly on the inside of the basket to keep them hidden.
11
Trim excess paper. Cut off any extra length from the vertical strips and tuck or glue the ends inside the basket for a clean finish.
12
Let the basket dry. Allow the glue to dry completely before using the basket to ensure it holds its shape and strength under weight.
