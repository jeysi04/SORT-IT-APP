How to Make a Bookmark Out of Old Folders

Bookmarks made from old folders are strong, reusable, and easy to customize. They are perfect for textbooks, notebooks, or novels and help reduce paper waste.

Materials Needed:

  • Old folder
  • Scissors
  • Marker or pen
  • Ruler and pencil (optional)
  • Hole punch and string or ribbon (optional)

Steps:

  1. Prepare the folder. Flatten the folder and place it on a table to ensure clean and straight cuts.
  2. Measure bookmark size. Mark long strips on the folder, about 4–5 cm wide and 15–20 cm long.
  3. Cut the bookmarks. Cut along the lines to form long, thin strips.
  4. Round the corners. Trim the corners slightly to prevent curling or damage when placed inside books.
  5. Decorate the bookmark. Write quotes, reminders, page labels, or draw simple designs using markers or pens.
  6. Add a tassel (optional). Punch a hole at the top of the bookmark. Thread a string or ribbon through the hole and tie a knot.
  7. Use the bookmark. Insert the bookmark into books or notebooks to mark pages easily.
