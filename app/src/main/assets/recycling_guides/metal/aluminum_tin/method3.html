How to Make a Makeup Brush Holder

Repurpose old aluminum cans or small containers into a stylish and functional holder for your makeup brushes. This keeps brushes upright, organized, and easy to access, while giving new life to items that would otherwise be discarded.

Materials Needed:

  • Aluminum can or small container
  • Mild soap and water
  • Sandpaper (optional)
  • Decorative paper, paint, or washi tape (optional)
  • Cotton balls, foam, or small beads (optional filler)

Steps:

  1. Clean the container. Wash the can or container thoroughly with mild soap and water. Remove labels or residue and dry completely.
  2. Smooth edges (if needed). Check the top rim for sharp edges. Use sandpaper or tape to prevent cuts.
  3. Decorate the container (optional). Wrap with decorative paper, paint, or washi tape to match your style.
  4. Add filler (optional). Place cotton balls, foam, or small beads inside the container to help brushes stand upright.
  5. Insert makeup brushes. Arrange brushes in the holder, keeping them organized and accessible.
