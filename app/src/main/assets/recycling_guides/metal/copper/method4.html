How to Make a Simple Circuit with LED

Instead of discarding old copper wires, you can repurpose them to build a simple LED circuit. This project teaches the basics of electricity and circuits while giving a second life to scrap copper wire, making it eco-friendly and educational.

Materials Needed:

  • Recycled copper wire (stripped, about 10–15 cm each)
  • 1 small LED
  • 1 battery (AA or 9V)
  • Electrical tape or small clips
  • Wire strippers or scissors (optional)
  • Gloves (optional, for handling wire ends)

Steps:

  1. Prepare the wires. Cut two short copper wires (10–15 cm). Strip about 1 cm of insulation from each end if needed.
  2. Check the LED legs. The longer leg is positive (+), the shorter leg is negative (–).
  3. Connect the first wire. Attach one wire from the battery’s positive (+) to the longer LED leg. Tape or clip it.
  4. Connect the second wire. Attach the other wire from the shorter LED leg to the battery’s negative (–). Secure it.
  5. Test it. The LED should light up if connected correctly.
  6. Try and learn. Flip the LED or battery to see how direction affects the light.
