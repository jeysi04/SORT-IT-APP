How to Make a Wire Jewelry Holder

Keep your rings, earrings, and necklaces organized with a stylish handmade wire jewelry holder. Using simple copper wire and a small wooden base, you can create a functional and decorative “tree” structure to neatly display your jewelry on your desk or vanity.

Materials Needed:

  • Copper wire (about 2–3 meters, 18–20 gauge recommended)
  • Pliers (needle-nose for precise bends)
  • Wire cutters
  • Small wooden block or base
  • Optional: Ribbon, beads, or paint for decoration

Steps:

  1. Cut and shape the wire. Using wire cutters, cut a length of copper wire (about 2–3 meters). Form multiple loops or branches with the wire using pliers. These will act as hooks for your jewelry.
  2. Form the tree structure. Twist the wire loops together at the base to create a “tree” shape. Adjust the branches so they spread out evenly and can hold jewelry without tangling.
  3. Attach to the base. Secure the bottom of the wire tree into a small wooden block for stability. You can drill a small hole in the block to insert the wire or wrap the wire around a nail/screw embedded in the base.
  4. Optional: Decorate. Wrap ribbons around the branches, add beads, or paint the wooden base to give it a personalized touch.
  5. Uses: Organize rings, earrings, and necklaces neatly. Add a decorative element to your desk or vanity. Makes a lovely handmade gift for jewelry lovers.
