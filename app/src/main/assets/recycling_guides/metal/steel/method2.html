Mini Water Fountains using Old Pots and Pans

Old pots, pans, or bowls can be repurposed into a relaxing mini water fountain for your garden, patio, or balcony. This project gives new life to unused cookware while creating a calming water feature. With proper sealing and pump placement, the fountain can continuously circulate water in a simple, eco-friendly way.

Materials Needed:

  • Old pots, pans, or bowls
  • Waterproof silicone sealant
  • Small submersible water pump
  • Flexible tubing (usually included with the pump)
  • Decorative stones or pebbles
  • Water
  • Gloves (optional)

Steps:

  1. Plan the layout. Choose pots or pans of different sizes and arrange them so water flows from the top to the bottom. Make sure the structure is stable.
  2. Secure the structure. Apply waterproof silicone sealant to all joints and gaps. Press the pieces together firmly and let the sealant dry completely.
  3. Install the pump. Place the submersible pump in the bottom pot. Attach the tube and guide it upward so water comes out at the top container.
  4. Test the water flow. Fill the bottom pot with water and turn on the pump. Adjust the pots or tubing so water flows smoothly without spilling.
  5. Decorate the fountain. Add stones to hide the tubing and improve the appearance. You may also add small plants or decorations.
