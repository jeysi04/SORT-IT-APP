Scrap Metal Recycling (Industrial Recycling)

Steel is one of the most commonly recycled metals, and industrial recycling helps conserve natural resources while reducing energy consumption. Large, damaged, or rusted steel items can be safely recycled at licensed scrap yards. Even bent or heavily used steel is valuable for producing new metal products.

Materials Needed:

  • Steel items (old tools, appliances, construction scrap, etc.)
  • Basic cleaning supplies (brush, cloth, mild detergent)
  • Gloves (optional, for safety)
  • Tools for removing non-metal parts (screwdriver, pliers, wrench)

Steps:

  1. Remove non-metal parts. Take off plastic, rubber, or wooden handles.
  2. Brush off dirt, grease, or loose rust.
  3. Sort if needed. Separate steel types if the recycler requires it.
  4. Transport safely. Bring items to a licensed scrap yard; wear gloves.
  5. Recycle. The steel is weighed and accepted by weight, even if rusty.
  6. The steel is melted and reused to make new products.
