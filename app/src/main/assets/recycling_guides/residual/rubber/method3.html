How to Make DIY Doorstops

Rubber
Old tires, rubber mats, or heavy scraps can be repurposed into durable doorstops. This project is eco-friendly, prevents doors from slamming, and gives new life to unused materials.

Materials Needed:

  • Rubber scraps, old rubber slippers, or tire pieces
  • Scissors, utility knife, or saw
  • Sandpaper (optional)
  • Paint (optional)
  • Adhesive (optional, for layered designs)

Steps:

1
Prepare the material. Clean and dry the rubber or tire piece thoroughly to ensure it won't track dirt into the house.
2
Cut to shape. Trim the rubber into a wedge or triangular form so it can easily slide under a door. Ensure the edges are relatively even.
3
Layer the rubber (optional). If your rubber isn't thick enough, use adhesive to glue multiple wedge pieces together for extra height.
4
Decorate (optional). Paint or embellish the doorstop to match your home decor and let it dry completely.
5
Place and test. Slide the wedge under the door and adjust it for a secure, non-slip fit.
