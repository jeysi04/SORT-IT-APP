How to Make DIY Doorstops

Old tires, rubber mats, or heavy scraps can be repurposed into durable doorstops. This project is eco-friendly, prevents doors from slamming, and gives new life to unused materials.

Materials Needed:

  • Rubber scraps, old rubber slippers or tire pieces.
  • Scissors, utility knife, or saw (depending on material).
  • Sandpaper (optional, for smoothing edges).
  • Paint (optional, for decoration).
  • Adhesive (optional, for layered designs).

Steps:

  1. Prepare the material. Clean and smooth rubber, tire, or wood.
  2. Cut to shape. Trim into a wedge or triangular form and even edges.
  3. Decorate (optional). Paint or embellish and let dry.
  4. Place and test. Slide under the door and adjust for a secure fit.
