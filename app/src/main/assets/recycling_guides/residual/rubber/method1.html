Rubber Hair Ties

Rubber
Old rubber items such as bike inner tubes, elastic bands, or discarded rubber strips can be repurposed into durable and stretchy hair ties. This project is eco-friendly, easy to make, and provides hair accessories that last longer than standard store-bought options.

Materials Needed:

  • Old rubber strips or inner tubes
  • Scissors or utility knife
  • Needle and thread (optional)
  • Sandpaper (optional)
  • Ruler (optional)

Steps:

1
Prepare the rubber. Clean and dry rubber strips or inner tubes. Trim damaged or thick sections.
2
Cut to size. Cut strips 1–2 cm wide and long enough to wrap around hair. Optional: Sand edges for a smoother finish.
3
Form the loop. Fold the strip into a loop and slightly overlap the ends to prepare for sealing.
4
Secure the ends. Stitch, knot, or glue the ends together securely depending on the thickness of the rubber.
5
Finish and test. Check stretch and comfort. Trim any rough edges. The hair tie is now ready to use.
