How to Turn Used Clothes into Rags

Old clothes, sheets, towels, or curtains can be easily repurposed into rags for cleaning and household use. This reduces textile waste and replaces disposable wipes with reusable alternatives.

Materials Needed:

  • Used clothes or textiles.
  • Scissors or rotary cutter.

Steps:

  1. Prepare the textiles. Select clean, dry fabrics. Wash and dry if needed.
  2. Cut into rags. Cut the fabric into convenient sizes for cleaning or wiping.
  3. Sort by use. Separate rags for kitchen, bathroom, or outdoor cleaning.
  4. Use and wash. Use rags for dusting, wiping, or scrubbing. Wash and reuse as needed.
