DIY Tote Bags from Used Clothes

Old clothes, curtains, or bedsheets can be upcycled into reusable tote bags. This project reduces textile waste and creates eco-friendly bags for shopping, school, or daily use.

Materials Needed:

  • Used clothes or fabric textiles.
  • Scissors.
  • Needle and strong thread or sewing machine.
  • Pins (optional).
  • Ruler or measuring tape (optional).

Steps:

  1. Prepare the fabric. Wash and dry the fabric. Cut into two equal rectangles for the bag body.
  2. Sew the bag body. Place fabric pieces together with patterned sides facing inward. Sew along the sides and bottom.
  3. Create the handles. Cut two long fabric strips. Fold, sew, and turn them right-side out to form handles.
  4. Attach the handles. Sew handles securely to the top edges of the bag. Reinforce stitching for strength.
  5. Finish the bag. Turn the bag right-side out. Trim loose threads. The tote bag is ready to use.
