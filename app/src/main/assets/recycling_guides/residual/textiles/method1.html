Donate Usable Clothing

Gently used clothes, shoes, and accessories can be donated to help others while keeping them out of landfills. Donating supports charities, thrift stores, and community programs.

Materials Needed:

  • Used clean clothes.

Steps:

  1. Sort the items. Select clean, dry, and wearable clothing. Separate by type if needed.
  2. Fold or bag. Neatly fold or place items in bags for easy handling.
  3. Donate. Bring items to thrift stores, charity organizations, or local clothing drives.
