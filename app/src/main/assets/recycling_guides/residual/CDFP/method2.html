Create Bags Out of Tarpaulin

Clean and Dry Flexible Plastics
Old advertising tarps and banner tarpaulins are made from strong, waterproof material, making them ideal for reusable bags and carry items. Repurposing them reduces plastic waste while creating durable, long-lasting bags for daily use.

Materials Needed:

  • Used tarpaulin or banner
  • Mild soap and water
  • Scissors or utility cutter
  • Needle and strong thread or sewing machine
  • Heat source for sealing edges (iron with cloth, heat gun)
  • Rope, webbing, or old straps for handles
  • Pins or clips (optional)

Steps:

1
Clean the tarpaulin. Wash the tarp with soap and water to remove dirt, dust, and ink residue. Rinse well and allow it to dry completely.
2
Plan and cut the design. Decide the size and shape of the bag you want (tote, tool, or travel bag). Mark the outline and cut the tarpaulin into the desired shapes.
3
Seal or sew the edges. Sew along the edges using strong thread for durability. Alternatively, carefully heat-seal the edges to prevent fraying (place cloth between the tarp and iron).
4
Assemble the bag. Join the sides and bottom securely by sewing or heat-sealing. Reinforce corners and stress points.
5
Add handles or straps. Attach rope, webbing, or old straps as handles. Sew them securely or heat-seal if the material allows.
6
Finish and test. Check seams and handles for strength. Test the bag by carrying light items first before regular use.
