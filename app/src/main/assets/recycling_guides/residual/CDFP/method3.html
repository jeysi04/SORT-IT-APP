How to Make an Outdoor Covers and Protectors from Tarpaulin

Clean and Dry Flexible Plastics
Thick tarpaulins are highly durable and water-resistant, making them perfect for outdoor covers and protectors. Reusing old tarps helps extend their lifespan while protecting equipment and materials from rain, dust, and sun exposure.

Materials Needed:

  • Thick used tarpaulin
  • Mild soap and water
  • Scissors or utility cutter
  • Duct tape or strong thread
  • Needle or sewing machine
  • Rope or bungee cords
  • Grommets and grommet tool (optional)

Steps:

1
Clean the tarpaulin. Wash the tarp with soap and water to remove dirt and debris. Rinse thoroughly and allow it to dry completely.
2
Measure and cut. Measure the item or area to be covered. Cut the tarpaulin slightly larger to allow proper coverage and securing.
3
Reinforce the edges. Fold edges inward and stitch them, or reinforce with duct tape to prevent tearing. For long-term use, install grommets along the edges if available.
4
Secure the cover. Use rope, bungee cords, or ties through reinforced edges or grommets. Ensure the cover is snug but not overly tight to avoid stress on the material.
5
Use as a ground sheet (optional). Lay the tarpaulin flat on the ground. Fold edges slightly inward to prevent water pooling.
