Woven Zipper Pouch using Plastic Wrappers

Clean and Dry Flexible Plastics
Colorful candy wrappers or shampoo sachets can be folded and woven together to create a durable, waterproof fabric. This technique is perfect for making unique coin purses, pencil cases, or small bags.

Materials Needed:

  • Clean candy wrappers or shampoo sachets
  • Scissors and a Ruler
  • Clear tape
  • Needle and thread (or sewing machine)
  • Zipper

Steps:

1
Clean and dry. Thoroughly wash the wrappers or sachets to remove any sticky residue or soap. They must be completely dry before you start folding.
2
Cut to uniform sizes. Use a ruler and scissors to cut all the wrappers into identically sized rectangles (e.g., 2 by 4 inches).
3
Fold into links. Fold the long edges of a rectangle to the center, then fold in half lengthwise. Fold the ends to the center again, and fold in half to create a small "V" shaped link.
4
Interlock the pieces. Slide the prongs of one "V" into the slots of another "V". Continue interlocking them to form a long, zigzagging chain. Make several chains of equal length.
5
Sew the rows. Line up your woven chains side-by-side. Use a needle and strong thread (or clear tape) to stitch the chains together, creating a solid sheet of woven plastic.
6
Attach a zipper. Fold the woven sheet in half to form a pouch shape. Sew the sides together, then hand-stitch a zipper to the top opening to finish your recycled coin purse!
