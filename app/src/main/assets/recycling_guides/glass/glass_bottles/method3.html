How to Make Decorative Glass Bottles

Reuse empty glass bottles to create simple and attractive decorations for your home. This easy project is a great way to recycle while adding a personal touch to your space.

Materials Needed

  • Clean glass bottles
  • Decorative fillers (sand, beads, shells, flowers, or lights)
  • Funnel (optional)
  • Ribbon, twine, or stickers (optional)
  • Scissors

Steps

  1. Clean and dry the bottles.
  2. Choose your decorative filler.
  3. Add the filler using a funnel if needed.
  4. Close or leave the bottle open.
  5. Decorate the outside (optional).
  6. Display your bottle.
  7. Add fairy lights for extra effect (optional).
