How to Clean Glass Bottles

Glass bottles can be recycled many times without losing quality. Follow these steps to recycle glass properly.

Materials Needed

  • Used glass bottles or jars
  • Warm water
  • Soap

Steps

  1. Empty the bottle and rinse it with water.
  2. Remove labels by soaking in warm water.
  3. Wash thoroughly using soap.
  4. Let it dry completely.

💡 Tip: Glass can also be reused as containers or decorations.
