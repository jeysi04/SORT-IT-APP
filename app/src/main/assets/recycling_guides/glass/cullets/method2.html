DIY Glass Mulch for Potted Plants

Cullet Glass
Recycled cullet glass can be used as decorative mulch for indoor or outdoor potted plants. It helps retain soil moisture, prevents weed growth, and adds a colorful, modern aesthetic to your garden containers.

Materials Needed:

  • Clean cullet glass fragments
  • Heavy-duty bag (burlap or canvas)
  • Hammer or mallet
  • Sandpaper or a rock tumbler (optional)
  • Thick gloves and safety glasses

Steps:

1
Clean the cullet. Wash all glass pieces to remove food residue or dust. Ensure they are completely dry before proceeding.
2
Crush safely. Place the glass inside a heavy-duty bag. Use a hammer to gently break the glass into small, pebble-sized pieces while wearing protective gear.
3
Dull the edges. Shake the bag vigorously for several minutes to allow the pieces to rub against each other, or manually rub sharp edges against sandpaper.
4
Rinse again. Place the crushed glass in a sieve and rinse it one more time to remove any tiny slivers or glass dust created during crushing.
5
Apply to plants. Spread a 1-inch layer of the glass cullet over the surface of the soil in your potted plants, leaving a small gap around the plant stem.
