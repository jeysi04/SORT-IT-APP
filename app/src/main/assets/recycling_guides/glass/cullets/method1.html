How to Recycle Cullet Glass

Cullet glass is broken glass that can be recycled into new glass products when handled properly.

Materials Needed

  • Cullet glass
  • Gloves
  • Container or box
  • Cardboard or thick paper

Steps

  1. Identify clean cullet glass. Ensure the glass is free from food, dirt, or other contaminants.
  2. Wear protective gloves. This helps prevent cuts while handling broken glass.
  3. Sort by type if required. Separate cullet by color or glass type, following local recycling rules.
  4. Wrap sharp pieces. Place cardboard or thick paper around large or sharp fragments.
  5. Place in a sturdy container. Use a rigid box or container to prevent punctures.
  6. Secure the container. Tape it closed to keep glass pieces from spilling.
  7. Label the container. Mark it as “Cullet Glass” or “Broken Glass” for safety.
  8. Transport carefully. Keep the container upright during transport.
  9. Deliver to a recycling facility. Bring the cullet glass to a center that accepts broken glass for recycling.
